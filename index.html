Our kunafa story

Kunafa began in the city of Nabulus, where bakers pressed fresh white cheese between layers of fine pastry and soaked it in sugar syrup while it was still hot. That simple idea has delighted families across the Middle East for centuries. We brought it home to Karachi.

Our kitchen follows the Nabulsi method: the cheese is soaked to remove excess salt, the kataifi is hand-shredded and buttered, and every tray is baked in small batches so the crust is crisp when it reaches you.

Karachi loves sweets, from late-night street desserts to grand wedding spreads, and kunafa fits every one of those moments. That is why we serve single portions for a quiet evening and full live stations for a thousand guests.

We use halal ingredients, real butter and Iranian pistachios, and we never reheat yesterday's kunafa. If it is not fresh, we do not serve it.
